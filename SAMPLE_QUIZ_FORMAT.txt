Quiz Questions - Chapter 5: Photosynthesis

1. What is the primary function of photosynthesis in plants?
A) To break down glucose for energy
B) To convert light energy into chemical energy stored in glucose
C) To produce oxygen for respiration only
D) To absorb water from the soil
Answer: B

2. Which of the following is NOT a requirement for photosynthesis?
A) Chlorophyll
B) Water
C) Carbon dioxide
D) Nitrogen
Answer: D

3. During which phase of photosynthesis is glucose produced?
A) Light-dependent reactions
B) Light-independent reactions (Calvin Cycle)
C) Electron transport chain
D) Water splitting
Answer: B

4. What is the role of chlorophyll in photosynthesis?
A) To transport water through the plant
B) To absorb light energy
C) To produce oxygen
D) To decompose carbon dioxide
Answer: B

5. Which wavelength of light is most effectively absorbed by chlorophyll a?
A) Red and blue light
B) Green light
C) Yellow light
D) Infrared light
Answer: A
